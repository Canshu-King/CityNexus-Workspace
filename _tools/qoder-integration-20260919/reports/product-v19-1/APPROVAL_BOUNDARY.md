# Approval Boundary

CityNexus remains the authority for hard capability confirmation, feasibility, protected commitments, booking/payment mutation, and PlanVersion truth. DeepSeek may interpret language and Hermes may orchestrate when available, but neither may invent provider accessibility facts or bypass Gateway/Guardian authorization.

Capability confirmation is explicit. Full replacement uses `PUT`; incremental confirmation uses `POST /confirm`, preserving previously active capability records. Hard accessibility needs cannot be silently downgraded.
